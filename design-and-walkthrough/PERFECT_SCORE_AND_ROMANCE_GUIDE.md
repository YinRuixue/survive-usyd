# Survive USYD — 100 Points + Successful Romance

> **Spoiler warning:** this guide reveals weekly choices, the confession requirement, Alex's contribution and the ending route.

**Verified:** 30 October 2026 · activity balance version 2  
**Goal:** midterm 100 + group report 100 + final exam 100, followed by the successful romance ending.  
**Applies to:** a fresh new game, female player/Noah or male player/Chloe. The same plan works with **all ten random wheel rewards**; no reroll is needed.

[Project and launch instructions](../README.md) · [Design and numerical rules](GAME_DESIGN.md)

## Before starting

Enter a character name. In this guide, **Partner** means **Hang out with Noah** for the female player or **Hang out with Chloe** for the male player. Use the partner outing rather than Cassy for the three specified dates: Cassy gives the same resource benefits but does not add romantic Intimacy.

Follow the three slots in the order shown. Buy only the three listed hot meals, **before opening/confirming that week's plan**. Follow the story choices below and continue through ordinary dialogue/status pages normally.

This route was checked from a new game. An older save with different prior choices may need a different plan. Save before important decisions if you want to explore alternatives.

## 1. O-Week and the first dinner

1. Spin the wheel once and accept whichever prize you receive.
2. Choose **TURN LEFT**, then **TAKE THE STAIRS**.
3. At the student-card decision, choose **ASK SOMEONE**.
4. Accept the contact exchange: **SURE, SEND IT!**
5. In Week 1, accept **DINNER TOGETHER · AU$15**.
6. Continue through the lecture and return to the dorm for the first weekly plan.

The contact exchange unlocks the romance route. The Week 1 dinner supplies the first Intimacy point and does not use one of your three weekly slots.

## 3. Key scenes in detail

### Week 2 — save the dinner money

Meet Cassy, form the group and continue the Coles scenes. When offered a marked-down meal, choose **STICK TO MY LIST**, not the AU$12 dinner. The route reserves that money for later recovery.

### Week 6 — play briefly, then sleep

After the weekly plan:

1. Choose **PLAY FOR A BIT** to reduce Stress.
2. At the next choice, choose **LOG OFF AND SLEEP**.
3. Continue through attending class on time and returning to the dorm.

Do not choose **ONE MORE GAME**. Logging off avoids the oversleeping branch and retains the on-time class's **Knowledge +8**.

### Week 9 — get all six Intimacy points

After the plan, the three partner outings from Weeks 2–4 qualify you for the balloon invitation.

1. Accept: **I'D LOVE TO**.
2. During the balloon conversation choose **LOOK AHEAD. THE SUN'S COMING UP.**
3. Continue to the dorm conversation that evening.
4. Choose **TELL HIM I LIKE HIM** for Noah, or the corresponding confession option for Chloe.

Your Intimacy is now:

| Source | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Week 1 dinner | 1 |
| Partner outing in Week 2 | 1 |
| Partner outing in Week 3 | 1 |
| Partner outing in Week 4 | 1 |
| Accepting/completing the balloon ride | 1 |
| Supportive balloon response | 1 |
| **Total** | **6** |

Six is sufficient for a successful confession. The balloon invitation is free. The other two balloon responses do not give the extra point, so this exact plan needs the supportive answer.

### Weeks 10–12 — secure the full report

In Week 10, after the midterm and the return to the dorm, choose **ASK ALEX FOR AN UPDATE**. This enables the tutor decision next week. Continue to check your midterm mark.

At the start of Week 11, choose **CONTACT THE TUTOR**. Then use the two Group Work slots in the table. At the end of the week, Alex's file contributes 10%.

In Week 12, buy the hot meal and complete **Group Work → Rest → Gaming**. Submit only after that plan has executed. The report total is:

```text
Your six Group Work slots: 6 × 10 = 60
Cassy:                              30
Alex after tutor contact:           10
Total:                             100
```

The six Group Work slots are in Weeks **5, 7, 8, 11, 11 and 12**. Reaching 90% in Week 11 is enough to pass, but it is not enough for a 100-point semester. Keep the Week 12 slot.

If you skip the Week 10 follow-up, or wait instead of contacting Ethan, Alex will not supply that 10%. This guide's six slots would then yield only 90%; do not make that substitution when following this route.

## 4. Finals and the ending

After the Week 13 presentation and summary:

1. Enter finals week through **Final exam ahead**.
2. On the dorm menu, choose **Go to Exam**, then confirm **GO TO EXAM**.
3. Continue through the campus walk, classroom, exam and stat-change page.
4. Return to the dorm and select **Next day**. No additional takeaway is required.
5. Continue to results. Cassy asks about your score; Alex later celebrates in the group and Cassy messages you privately.
6. On **Academic Legend**, select **Continue** to see the romance epilogue and harbour-date CG.
7. **Start new game** appears at the end of **A Day Together**.

The academic ending must lead to Continue because the confession succeeded. If it shows Start new game instead, check the earlier contact-exchange and confession choices.

## 6. Why the final score is 100

```text
Midterm:      100 × 25% = 25
Group report: 100 × 25% = 25
Final exam:   100 × 50% = 50
Semester result:        100
```

The route exceeds the Knowledge thresholds, leaves enough Energy and low enough Stress when exam marks are captured, and submits a complete report. Romance succeeds independently through six Intimacy points.

A 90% report would reduce the total to 97.5 even with perfect exams. Academic HD and Academic Legend are different endings.

## 7. Avoid these deviations

- **Do not choose three identical Study, Rest, Gaming, Work or Exercise activities in one week.** Each triggers a special ending if resource failure has not already stopped the run.
- **Do not replace the three partner outings with Cassy outings.** The resource values match, but romantic Intimacy does not.
- **Do not delay all partner outings until Week 9.** Balloon eligibility counts outings completed by the end of Week 8.
- **Do not replace Gaming with Rest throughout the guide.** Sleep is not a substitute for Stress relief in this balance version.
- **Do not skip the three hot meals or buy extra meals.** Follow the specified weeks and buy before the plan.
- **Do not keep gaming late in the Week 6 event.** The guide relies on the on-time class reward.
- **Do not stop Group Work at 90%.** The remaining Week 12 contribution is required for full marks.
- **Do not expect rest after the final to repair an exam mark.** Marks are captured when the exam is completed.

All twenty runs reached Academic Legend, the successful romance epilogue, and the normal new-game exit. This is one verified route, not the only possible winning strategy.
